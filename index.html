How to Use This Site

The Escalation Project tracks geopolitical conflict events chronologically on an interactive timeline.

Gamified "What If": Drag and drop available cards from the side decks into the top dropzone to simulate future outcomes!

Point System: Click the yellow ⭐ Score button on any card to award 0-10 points based on strategic impact. (Default is 10 points).

Leverage Meter & Map View: The animated dashboard updates in real-time. Use the top right Map button to see a geographic plot of events.
